Voice

Plum, the voice assistant

Open Plum from the menu and say what you want to watch, in English, German or Croatian. It searches the whole playlist, including the TV guide and the provider’s catch-up archive, and opens what you meant.

Install

Amazon Fire TV Stick

  1. On the Fire TV home screen, search for Downloader and install it (free).
  2. Open Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options and allow apps from unknown sources for Downloader. No Developer options? Go to My Fire TV → About and press OK on the device name 7 times.
  3. Open Downloader, type this address and press Go: {{ links.short }}
  4. Install, open Plumcast and add your playlist. You can delete the downloaded file afterwards.

Works on Fire OS 6 and newer (most sticks since 2018). The newest Fire TV models with Vega OS can't install Android apps. Voice (Plum) isn't available on Fire TV, everything else is.

Android TV / Google TV

Same as Fire TV: install Downloader from Google Play, allow it to install apps, and open {{ links.short }}.

Android phone

Tap Download for Android, open the file and allow your browser to install apps when asked.

No content included

Plumcast is only a player. It comes without channels, playlists or links, and I don't sell or recommend any provider. Use it with a playlist you are allowed to watch. Your login stays on your device: no account, no tracking, no servers of mine in between.
